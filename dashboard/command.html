OWNER WORKFLOW

What happens after you give a command

1. Capture

Your request becomes a structured project brief.

2. Validate

Research and feasibility workers investigate the opportunity.

3. Plan

Project Manager creates the execution plan and worker tasks.

4. Build

Workers execute through the Control Plane.

5. Measure

Operations and Finance track progress, costs and revenue evidence.

6. Approve

You only step in for consequential decisions, especially money movement.

IMPORTANT

Leverage is a company system, not a trading system

Projects are replaceable workloads. Workers and Control Plane rules are reusable company infrastructure. Trading is currently parked; future projects use the same intake, validation, execution, measurement and revenue pipeline.
