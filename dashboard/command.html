Your request becomes a structured project brief.

2. Validate

3. Plan

4. Build

5. Measure

6. Approve

You only step in for consequential decisions, especially money movement.

IMPORTANT

The dashboard is your visual control room. ChatGPT remains the natural-language manager; the Control Plane remains the execution authority. This keeps the Boss experience simple without exposing secrets or giving a browser page unrestricted system access.
