Research checklist

Before believing a strategy result.

VALIDATION GATES
1
Data qualityVerify date range, missing candles, timezone and market type.
2
Trading costsConfirm fees, spread and slippage are included realistically.
3
Out-of-sampleTest on unseen data that was not used to tune rules.
4
RobustnessCheck walk-forward results and nearby parameter values.
5
Risk & executionReview drawdown, losing streaks, position sizing and fill assumptions.
6
Paper tradeForward-test before considering any real capital.
