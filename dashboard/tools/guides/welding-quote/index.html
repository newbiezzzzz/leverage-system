How to Build a Welding Job Quote

A welding quote should cover preparation, welding time, consumables, cleanup, overhead and the required profit. The exact cost structure varies by shop and job.

Start with time

Separate setup and preparation time from active welding time. Include handling, fit-up, inspection and cleanup where applicable.

Include consumables

Estimate gas, wire or electrodes, grinding discs and other job-specific consumables rather than treating them as invisible costs.

Add overhead and profit

Apply the shop's cost-based hourly rate and pricing policy, then check the resulting gross margin against the target.
