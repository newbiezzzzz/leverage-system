Markup vs Margin

Markup and margin are related but not interchangeable. Markup is measured against cost; margin is measured against selling price.

Formulas

Markup = (Selling Price - Cost) / Cost.

Margin = (Selling Price - Cost) / Selling Price.

Why it matters

Using markup when you mean margin can produce a different selling price than expected. Always define which measure your quote or pricing policy uses.
