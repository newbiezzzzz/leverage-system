How to Calculate Job Profit

Job profit is the revenue from the job less the costs required to complete it. Separate estimated costs from actual costs so quoting performance can be reviewed after delivery.

Simple formula

Job Profit = Job Revenue - Total Job Cost.

Total job cost can include direct labor, material, consumables, subcontracting and an allocated share of overhead.

Compare quote to actuals

The useful management signal is not just the final profit. Compare the original estimate with actual hours, material use and other costs to identify where future quotes need adjustment.
