How to Calculate a Fabrication Shop Hourly Rate

A practical shop rate starts with annual operating costs and divides them by realistic productive hours, then adds the target margin or markup required by the business.

Basic method

Estimate annual fixed costs, direct labor burden and other operating costs. Then estimate productive billable hours, not just paid hours. Divide total annual cost by productive hours to get a cost-based hourly rate.

Why productive hours matter

A shop cannot normally bill every paid hour. Downtime, quoting, maintenance, rework, setup, purchasing and administration reduce productive capacity.
