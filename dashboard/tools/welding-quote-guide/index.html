How to Estimate a Welding Job Quote

A welding quote should reflect the complete job, not only arc time. Preparation, fit-up, consumables, setup, cleanup and inspection can materially change the cost.

Estimate preparation

Account for cutting, fit-up, tack welding, fixtures and setup before welding starts.

Estimate welding and consumables

Use realistic welding hours and include wire, electrodes, shielding gas and other consumables.

Add overhead and profit

Recover an appropriate share of shop overhead and apply a clear markup or margin target.
