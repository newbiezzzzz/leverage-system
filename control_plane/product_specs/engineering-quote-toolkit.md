# Fabrication Shop Profit & Quote System — v1

## Product type
Digital product: macro-free Excel workbook with repeatable digital delivery. No custom service is required.

## Target buyer
Small fabrication shops, welding businesses, machine/job shops, and engineering workshops that quote jobs using material + labour + outside-service costs but do not need full estimating software.

## Buyer problem
Owners can lose margin because they underestimate shop rate, forget consumables/overhead, apply inconsistent markup, or never compare estimated job economics with actual results.

## Core promise
**Calculate the real job cost, build a defensible quote, and learn where profit leaked after the job.**

## Product modules
1. **Shop Rate Calculator** — labour, machine and overhead assumptions.
2. **Quote Builder** — material, labour, outside service and consumable lines.
3. **Profit Protection** — target gross margin, overhead allowance, expected profit.
4. **Actual vs Estimate Job Log** — quoted sales, actual cost, actual profit and margin.
5. **Change Order Register** — approved change value, added cost and revised profit.
6. **Consumables & Waste Allowance** — explicit allowance for welding/fabrication consumables and material waste.
7. **Quick Start** — worked sample job and instructions.

## Differentiation hypothesis
- Built around **profit protection**, not just quote formatting.
- Niche wording for fabrication/welding/machine-shop workflows.
- Simple workbook rather than a full shop-management platform.
- No VBA/macros and no external service dependency.
- Lets a shop learn from quoted-vs-actual results instead of treating each quote as a one-off.

## Deliverables
- Fabrication_Shop_Profit_Quote_System_v1.xlsx
- Quick_Start_Guide.pdf
- Sample_Job_Data.csv
- Gumroad listing copy and preview images

## Pricing hypothesis
Launch at **US$19**. This is deliberately below broader paid shop quoting/job-costing packs observed in the market, while preserving room to test a higher price after real buyer feedback. Competitor pricing is evidence of willingness-to-pay, not proof that Leverage's product will sell.

## Distribution
Primary storefront: Gumroad.

Initial traffic strategy:
- search-driven product keywords;
- useful educational posts around shop rates, quoting and profit leakage;
- relevant communities where promotional posting is permitted;
- direct Gumroad product link.

Do not depend on Gumroad Discover for first-sale validation. The first-sale path must be capable of working from external/direct traffic.

## Success gate
Build → verify artifact → publish → obtain the first real external transaction. Revenue remains zero until Gumroad reports a verified sale.

## Product boundaries
- Customer supplies their own rates, tax assumptions and business rules.
- Not accounting, tax, legal or engineering certification advice.
- No macros/VBA.
- No customer-specific implementation included in the base product.

## Evidence
Current market research recorded in `control_plane/product_specs/first_product_selection.json` shows paid welding/fabrication/job-costing products, including listings with reviews/sales and a Gumroad machine-shop quoting pack. The selected product remains a candidate for first-sale validation, not a guaranteed winner.
