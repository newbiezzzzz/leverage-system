# Engineering Quote & Job-Costing Toolkit — v1

## Positioning
A macro-free Excel toolkit for small engineering, fabrication and machine-shop businesses that need faster quoting and a clearer view of job margin without buying full estimating software.

## Buyer
- Small fabrication / welding shops
- Machine shops and job shops
- Engineering workshops
- Small contractors doing material + labour + outside-service quotes

## Core promise
Estimate a job, protect target margin, and compare quoted vs actual cost in one reusable workbook.

## Included
1. Quote Builder
2. Rates & Assumptions
3. Job Log — quoted vs actual
4. Quick-start instructions
5. Sample line categories: material, labour, outside service

## Product constraints
- No macros / VBA
- No external API
- One-time digital purchase
- Buyer supplies own rates and assumptions
- Not accounting, tax or legal advice

## Launch price decision
**US$9.99 introductory price**.

Rationale: current comparable products observed in the market include roughly US$19.97–US$34 for broader quoting/job-costing packs, with simpler single-workbook products around US$29 and niche CNC calculators much higher. Leverage v1 is intentionally narrower, so the launch price is set below those broader packs until buyer feedback validates differentiation.

## Differentiation hypothesis
- Specifically worded for engineering / fabrication / machine-shop users
- Simple, low-friction workbook instead of a complex workshop-management system
- No macros
- Focuses on quoting + margin + job learning rather than invoicing or inventory
- Designed to expand from one practical workbook into a reusable product line

## Validation evidence
- Gumroad machine-shop quoting pack: US$34; includes quote worksheet, shop rate calculator and job cost recap.
- Etsy job-costing template: example listing uses automated materials/labour/markup/profit workflow.
- Gumroad CNC job-shop quoting calculator: US$249, showing higher willingness to pay for specialised quoting tools.
- Planovate Pro estimating/planning toolkit: US$19.97, Excel estimator with labour/materials/job planner.

## Next gate
Before increasing price or adding a large feature set, obtain real buyer feedback and first-sale evidence. Do not treat competitor pricing as proof of demand for this product.
