## FAQ
**Does it use macros?** No. The workbook is macro-free.

**Does it need an API or internet connection?** No.

**Can I use my own rates?** Yes. The Shop Rate sheet is intended to be configured with the buyer's own business inputs.

**Is this accounting software?** No. It is a quoting/job-costing tool and does not provide accounting, tax or legal advice.
