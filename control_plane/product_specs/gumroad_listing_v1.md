## Short description
**Know the cost and margin before you quote.** A macro-free Excel toolkit for small fabrication, welding and machine/job shops to calculate shop rates, build quotes, include consumables and overhead, compare quoted vs actual job economics, and track change orders.

## Who it is for
- Small fabrication shops
- Welders and mobile welders
- Machine/job shops
- Engineering workshops that price fabrication work
- Small contractors with material + labour + outside-service costing

## The problem it solves
Small shops can lose margin through an unrealistic shop rate, forgotten consumables, inconsistent markup, hidden overhead, or never comparing the original estimate with the final result. This workbook gives those steps one repeatable workflow.

## What is included
- **Shop Rate Calculator** — labour, machine/equipment and overhead assumptions.
- **Quote Builder** — material, labour, consumables and outside-service lines.
- **Profit Protection** — target margin, overhead allowance, expected quote and gross profit check.
- **Job Log** — quoted sales versus actual cost, actual profit and margin.
- **Change Order Register** — added value, added cost and revised profit.
- **Sample Job Data** — example structure for learning the workflow.
- **Quick-start guide** — concise setup and usage instructions.

## FAQ
**Does it use macros?** No. The workbook is macro-free.

**Does it need an API or internet connection?** No. The workbook is designed to run locally in Excel-compatible software.

**Can I use my own rates?** Yes. Replace the example assumptions with your own verified business inputs.

**Is this accounting software?** No. It is a quoting/job-costing tool and is not accounting, tax, legal or engineering certification advice.

**Is a subscription required?** No subscription is required to use the workbook itself.
