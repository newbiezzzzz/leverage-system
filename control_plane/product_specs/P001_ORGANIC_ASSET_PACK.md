# P-001 Organic Asset Pack v1

## Asset 01 — Problem / solution
**Hook:** Your quote can be correct on paper and still lose money.
**Body:** The usual leaks are an unrealistic shop rate, forgotten consumables, hidden overhead and markup that changes from job to job. A repeatable quote workflow makes each cost visible before the number goes to the customer.
**CTA:** See the Fabrication Shop Profit & Quote System.

## Asset 02 — Shop-rate explainer
**Hook:** Your hourly shop rate is not just wages.
**Body:** A practical starting structure is labour burden + machine/equipment recovery + overhead burden. Use realistic productive hours, not theoretical available hours, or the rate will under-recover the shop.
**CTA:** See how the workbook structures shop-rate assumptions.

## Asset 03 — Quote workflow
**Hook:** Five steps from job request to profit review.
**Body:** Shop Rate → Quote → Profit Check → Job Log → Change Order. The point is not more paperwork. It is making the same economic checks repeatable.
**CTA:** View the workflow.

## Asset 04 — Profit-leak checklist
**Hook:** Before sending a fabrication quote, check these.
**Checklist:** labour hours • machine time • material waste • consumables • outside services • overhead allowance • target margin • scope changes.
**CTA:** Use the checklist, then compare it with the workbook.

## Asset 05 — Quote vs actual
**Hook:** The quote is only half the learning loop.
**Body:** After a job, compare quoted sales with actual material, labour, consumables, outside services and overhead. The gap tells you what your next quote should improve.
**CTA:** See the Job Log structure.

## Asset 06 — Change orders
**Hook:** Scope changes can quietly eat margin.
**Body:** Track added sales and added cost together. A change is not profitable just because the customer approved extra work.
