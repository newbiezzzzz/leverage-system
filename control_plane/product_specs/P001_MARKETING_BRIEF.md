# P-001 Marketing Creative Brief

## Audience
Owners/operators of small fabrication, welding, machine/job and metalworking shops who quote custom jobs.

## Core problem
They need to know whether a job is profitable before saying yes, and whether the actual result matched the quote.

## Creative angle
**Stop guessing your quote. See the cost, margin and profit before you commit.**

## Visual language
- Real workbook/UI evidence as the centerpiece.
- Industrial/technical visual cues without looking dated.
- Strong hierarchy and contrast.
- One idea per visual.
- Short headlines; detailed explanation in the caption/article.
- Consistent Leverage design system.

## First 10 organic assets
1. Problem/solution: why shops underquote.
2. Shop-rate explainer with a visual formula.
3. Before/after quote workflow visual.
4. Profit-leak checklist.
5. Quote vs actual mini case example.
6. Change-order margin protection visual.
7. Calculator demo script.
8. Macro-free / no-subscription positioning visual.
9. FAQ: what the workbook does and does not do.
10. Direct product introduction with the Gumroad link.

## Content rule
Lead with useful information. Every asset should be independently useful even when the product link is removed. No mass spam, fake proof, fake urgency or unverifiable claims.

## Conversion rule
Every promotional asset should have one obvious next action: learn more, see the calculator, or evaluate the product.
