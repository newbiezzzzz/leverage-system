# P-001 Public Landing Page — Fabrication Shop Profit & Quote System

## Goal
Turn qualified visitors from fabrication/welding/job-costing searches into confident product evaluators.

## Design direction
Industrial-professional rather than generic SaaS. Use clear workshop imagery or product UI evidence, strong type hierarchy, restrained technical styling, generous whitespace, and a premium-but-practical feel.

## Hero
Headline: **Quote fabrication jobs with the cost and margin visible before you commit.**
Subhead: A macro-free Excel toolkit for small fabrication, welding and machine shops to calculate shop rates, build quotes, protect margin, compare quoted vs actual results, and track change orders.
Primary CTA: **Get the Profit & Quote System**
Secondary CTA: **See how it works**

## Sections
1. Problem: underquoting, missed consumables, hidden overhead, inconsistent markup.
2. Workflow: Shop Rate -> Quote -> Profit Check -> Job Log -> Change Orders.
3. Product UI/screenshots: show the workbook as the evidence, not abstract stock graphics.
4. What is included: XLSX + quick-start guide + sample data.
5. Who it is for / not for.
6. Example scenario: material + labour + overhead -> target quote -> actual result.
7. FAQ: macros, internet, own rates, accounting boundaries.
8. Final CTA.

## SEO
Primary intent terms: fabrication quote calculator, welding quote calculator, fabrication estimating spreadsheet, job costing spreadsheet, welding shop rate calculator.
Use semantic variants naturally; do not keyword-stuff.

## Conversion rules
- Show the product before asking for the sale.
- Put proof/evidence beside claims.
- Do not use fake testimonials or fake customer counts.
- Keep the first CTA visible without intrusive popups.
- Mobile layout must remain readable and persuasive.

## Quality gate
Minimum creative/conversion score: 85/100.
